All six concepts

Mockups only — no firmware, no live data and nothing tested on hardware. Screens are drawn at the panel’s native 480 × 480 px. The panel is about 72 mm square, so 1 px ≈ 0.15 mm: primary touch targets are ≥ 64 px (≈ 10 mm) and nothing tappable is under 48 px (≈ 7 mm). Night mode assumes a dimmed backlight and a red-shifted palette; alarms override the dimming. This is a prototype concept, not a certified marine safety system.
